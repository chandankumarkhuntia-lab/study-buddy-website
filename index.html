Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Study Buddy is designed to support students in managing their academic resources. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information and Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app may receive basic account information such as your email address and profile information, subject to the permissions you approve.

Google Drive access

If you authorize Google Drive access, the app uses the permissions shown during Google's consent process to provide its file-related features. Review Google's consent screen before granting access.

How information is used

Information is used to support sign-in and the app features you choose to use. Do not upload passwords or other sensitive information into study files.

Storage and sharing

Information and files may be processed by services configured in the app, including Google services. The app operator should document any additional storage, analytics or sharing actually used by the app.

Data retention and deletion

Data retention depends on the services used and the files you choose to store. To request help with your data, contact the developer using the email below.

Children's privacy

Parents or guardians who have questions about a child's information may contact the developer.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
